FAQs

accordion
What kind of card is Citi Double Cash®?

The Citi Double Cash® Card is a Mastercard credit card that earns cash back as ThankYou® Points. Cardmembers earn 1% cash back on purchases and an additional 1% as they pay for those purchases.
How do you redeem rewards?

ThankYou® Points can be redeemed for cash back as a statement credit, direct deposit, or check. Other redemption options include gift cards, travel, and Shop with Points. Redemption rates may vary.
What is the APR for Citi Double Cash® Card?

The standard variable APR depends on a margin added to the prime rate and the applicant’s creditworthiness. Review the current pricing details before applying.
What is the credit limit?

As with any credit card, the credit limit is determined based on the cardholder’s creditworthiness.
How can I apply?

Use the Apply now link to continue to Citi’s secure application experience. Approval is subject to Citi’s terms and credit requirements.
Is there a limit to the cash back I can earn?

There is no cap on the amount of cash back you can earn and no category enrollment to track.
